Zaroori jaankari

    
Ye app classic Bluetooth (SPP) printers se judti hai, jo phone me pair hote hain. Printer ka protocol ESC/POS ya TSPL hona chahiye. Pehle Test print karke dekho. Sticker ulta ya khali nikle to 180° ulta ya Black/white ulta tick karo.

    
Web Bluetooth sirf BLE printers se jud sakta hai (Chrome Android/PC). Classic Bluetooth printers ke liye APK wali app use karo. Printer ka protocol ESC/POS ya TSPL hona chahiye.
